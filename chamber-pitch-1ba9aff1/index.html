A Member Video Program for Local Business

A partnership proposal prepared for the Benton–West City Area Chamber of Commerce — and adaptable for the West Frankfort Chamber of Commerce.

Reel Boost Media is a local, done-for-you short-video service based in Buckner, Illinois. We film real businesses, with real footage, and deliver post-ready promo videos every month — the work owners know they need but don't have time for. This proposal asks the Chamber to partner on a member program at no cost to the Chamber, so member businesses get professional video at small-town prices, and the towns they operate in get promoted alongside them.

01

What we're asking for

This is a partnership, not a sales pitch and not an investment ask. Five specific things — each small, each reversible.

1

A 20-minute meeting

With the board or executive director, to walk through this proposal and answer questions before anything is agreed.

2

A directory listing

Reel Boost Media listed in the Chamber's member directory like any other local business.

3

One member announcement

A single introduction of the member video program in the Chamber newsletter or on its social channels — wording approved by the Chamber first.

4

Introductions to 5–10 member businesses

Warm introductions to members who have said they struggle with social media, so the first pilots go to businesses that want them.

5

Event-calendar access

So event promos and hype videos line up with Chamber events instead of competing with them.

What this is not

  • Not a fee. The Chamber pays nothing for the partnership itself.
  • Not exclusive. The Chamber can work with any other video or marketing provider, any time.
  • Not a long-term contract. Either side can end the arrangement with a conversation.
  • Not an endorsement obligation. The Chamber endorses nothing beyond what it agrees to in writing.
  • Not a money pass-through. Members pay Reel Boost Media directly, only if they choose a pilot or plan.

08

Safeguards the Chamber can quote

If a board member asks “what if a video goes wrong?” — this is the answer, in writing, in every client agreement.

Real footage only

Every video is built on real footage of the real business. AI is our disclosed production method — it is never used to fake a review, a testimonial, a price, or a result. Federal rules (the FTC's 2024 rule on reviews and testimonials) ban exactly that, and we follow them.

Consent before filming

Written permission before any person is filmed, and the business owner approves the finished video before it is posted anywhere. No guaranteed views or sales claims — ever.

Licensed & documented

Music and production tools are used under commercial licenses, with receipts kept. A member's video will never put the member — or the Chamber's name — at legal risk.
